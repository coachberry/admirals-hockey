Get the Admirals Hockey App

Install the site on your phone for quick access to schedules, chat, and RSVPs

Get one-tap access to the schedule, roster, team chat, and RSVPs by adding Admirals Hockey to your phone's home screen. It works like an app — no App Store download needed.

🍎 iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Open Safari and go to fhsadmiralshockey.com — must be Safari, not Chrome, for notifications to work
  2. Tap the Share icon (square with an arrow pointing up) at the bottom of the screen
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
  4. Tap Add in the top right corner
  5. Open the app from the new icon on your Home Screen
  6. Log in and tap Allow when asked about notifications, so you never miss a game reminder or chat message

🤖 Android (Chrome)

  1. Open Chrome and go to fhsadmiralshockey.com
  2. Tap the three-dot menu in the top right corner
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app if that option appears)
  4. Tap Add or Install to confirm
  5. Open the app from the new icon on your Home Screen
  6. Log in and allow notifications so you get game reminders and chat alerts

💡 Why install it?

  • Opens instantly like a real app — no browser bar or typing the address
  • Get push notifications for game reminders, RSVP requests, and chat messages
  • Quick access to the schedule, roster, and team chat, right from your home screen
