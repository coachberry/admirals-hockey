My Roles
🏒 About Roles

Your role determines what you can access on the Admirals Hockey site. Here's what each role means:

Player — Current FHS Hockey roster member. Full access to team chat, events, and member features.
Prospect — Prospective player interested in trying out. Access to tryout info and basic member features.
Alumni — Former FHS Hockey player. Access to team chat and alumni features.
Coach — Coaching staff member. Full access to team features.
Team Rep — Team representative or manager. Access to team features and events.
Member — Summer league participant or other community member. Basic access.

All role requests are reviewed by Coach Berry. You'll keep your current access while your request is pending.
