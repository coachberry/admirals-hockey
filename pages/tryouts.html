🏒 Who Should Try Out?

We welcome players of all experience levels who attend or are eligible to attend Franklin High School. Whether you've been skating your whole life or are newer to the sport, we encourage you to come out and show us what you've got.

  • Current Franklin High School students in grades 9–12
  • Incoming freshmen for the 2026–27 school year
  • Returning players from last season's roster
  • Transfer students who meet TSSAA eligibility requirements
  • All positions — forwards, defensemen, and goaltenders
⭐ What to Expect

Tryouts are designed to evaluate skating ability, hockey sense, compete level, and coachability. We look for players who bring energy, work hard, and are committed to the team.

⛸️ Skating Edge work, speed, agility, and backward skating drills
🏒 Puck Skills Stickhandling, passing, and shooting evaluation
🎯 Compete Small-area games and competitive drills
📋 Results Roster decisions communicated TBD
❓ Frequently Asked Questions
Do I need to register before tryouts?
Yes — please complete the registration form on this page before attending tryouts. This helps us plan ice time and ensure we have your information on file.
I've never played organized hockey before. Can I still try out?
Absolutely. We evaluate players based on their potential and work ethic, not just experience. If you can skate and love the game, come out and give it a shot.
I played last season. Do I still need to register?
Yes — all players, returning and new, must complete the registration form and attend tryouts.
Who do I contact with questions?
Reach out to Coach Matt Berry at coachberry03@gmail.com
